Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Merge Spirits ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with your use of our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

We strive to collect as little personal information as possible. Depending on your use of the App, the following data may be processed:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, and unique device identifiers used for crash reporting and analytics.
  • Gameplay data: in-game progress, settings, scores, and preferences stored locally on your device. This data may be backed up via your device's platform services (e.g., Google Play Games, Apple Game Center, iCloud).
  • Diagnostic data: anonymous crash logs and performance metrics used to improve stability.
  • Advertising identifiers: if applicable, identifiers such as the Apple IDFA or Android Advertising ID may be processed by third-party ad partners (only with your consent where required by law).
  • Purchase information: if you make in-app purchases, the transaction is processed by the platform (Apple App Store or Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

We do not knowingly collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • operate, maintain, and improve the App;
  • save your game progress and preferences;
  • diagnose crashes and technical issues;
  • deliver, measure, and (where applicable) personalize advertising;
  • comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you. These providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they handle such information. Examples may include:

  • Google Play Services / Apple App Store
  • Firebase / Google Analytics (analytics and crash reporting)
  • AdMob, Unity Ads, or similar advertising networks (if ads are present)

We encourage you to review each provider's privacy policy.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

5. Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Anonymous diagnostic and analytics data is retained by our service providers in accordance with their retention policies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we process about you;
  • request correction or deletion of your data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.
